Rakuten Travel runs a monthly campaign called "Tokutabi" that gives you up to 15x points on hotel bookings. Most people just use those points for shopping — but there's a smarter move.

You can transfer your Rakuten Points to **Rakuten Securities** and use them to buy index funds, stocks, or even invest through a NISA tax-free account.

Here's how the whole flow works.

## The Points You Earn Are "Regular Points"

First, the important part: the bonus points from the Tokutabi campaign are **regular points** (not limited-time points). That means they're eligible for Rakuten Securities' point investment program.

Campaign details:

- Frequency: Monthly
- Entry: Required before booking
- Bonus rates: up to 10x for same-month or next-month stays, up to 15x for bookings 2+ months ahead
- Points awarded: About 10 days after checkout

## What Is Rakuten Securities Point Investment?

Rakuten Securities lets you use Rakuten Points at **1 point = 1 yen** to buy:

- Mutual funds / index funds (lump sum or recurring)
- Japanese stocks
- US stocks (yen settlement)
- NISA tax-free accounts

For beginners, **recurring purchases of a global index fund** is the easiest way to start.

## Step-by-Step

1. **Book a hotel** on Rakuten Travel (enter the Tokutabi campaign first)
2. **Check in and stay**
3. **Receive points** (~10 days after checkout)
4. **Set up point investment** on Rakuten Securities
5. **Buy an index fund**

### Preparing Your Rakuten Securities Account

If you don't have an account yet:

- Account opening: Free, fully online
- Maintenance: Free
- Point course: You need to switch to "Rakuten Point Course" in your account settings

### Recommended Funds

- Rakuten Whole World Index Fund (global diversification)
- Rakuten All-US Equity Index (S&P500 tracking)
- Rakuten Balance Fund (automatic diversification, good for beginners)

## Important Notes

- **Limited-time points cannot be used** for point investment. Only regular points qualify.
- Selling fund units purchased with points gives you cash (yen), not points.
- Point investment is available in NISA accounts — use it to maximize tax-free gains.
- There are upper limits on how many points you can invest per transaction.

## Summary

Turning travel rewards into investments is a simple shift that turns "spending" into "building assets." The Tokutabi campaign runs every month, so keep it in mind next time you book a trip.
